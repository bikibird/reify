Reify

Reify is a JavaScript frame work for writing interactive fiction (text adventure) applications for modern web browsers. It supports parser and choice based games equally well and even provides support for drag and drop interfaces. Reify is currently under development with a pre-alpha version expected January 2026. It's open source and licensed under the ISC license.

Reify provides an intuitive, almost English-like coding experience that keeps you immersed in your story world. Story elements are the persons, places, things, concepts, and traits that make your story world unique. Facts describe the relationships between the story elements. Scenes are triggered with facts change.
